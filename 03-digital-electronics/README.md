# Phase 3 — Digital Electronics

> **Goal:** Understand digital logic, Boolean algebra, logic gates, and digital circuit fundamentals.
>
> **Prerequisite:** Phase 1 - Computer Fundamentals
>
> **Outcome:** You can understand and design basic digital circuits and understand how digital logic works.

## What This Phase Will Cover

When complete, this phase will include:

1. **Digital vs Analog**
   - Digital signals
   - Logic levels
   - Noise immunity
   - Advantages of digital

2. **Number Systems in Digital**
   - Binary in digital systems
   - Hexadecimal for digital
   - BCD
   - Gray code

3. **Logic Gates**
   - AND, OR, NOT gates
   - NAND, NOR, XOR, XNOR
   - Truth tables
   - Gate symbols

4. **Boolean Algebra**
   - Boolean operations
   - Simplification
   - Karnaugh maps
   - Logic optimization

5. **Combinational Logic**
   - Adders
   - Multiplexers
   - Decoders
   - Encoders

6. **Sequential Logic**
   - Flip-flops
   - Latches
   - Counters
   - State machines

7. **Timing and Clocks**
   - Clock signals
   - Setup and hold times
   - Propagation delay
   - Metastability

## Prerequisites

- Phase 1 - Computer Fundamentals
- Basic algebra
